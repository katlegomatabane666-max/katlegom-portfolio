About

I was not handed a shortcut. I built this instead.

I am Katlego Khumo Matabane, born and based in Vosloorus on the East Rand. I finished Grade 11 at Sunward Park High School in 2022 and then N3 at Ekurhuleni West TVET College in 2024. No bursary, no laptop handed to me, no family business to walk into.

Today I am a Lead Call Centre Agent at Training Force, where I make high-volume outbound calls, present and close on Momentum Insurance products, and keep accurate records of every conversation. It taught me the two things that matter in any job: how to talk to people who do not want to talk to you, and how to hit a number every single day.

Outside of work I design and code websites. I taught myself HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and I do layout and graphics work in Photoshop and Illustrator. I will not pretend to be a senior developer — I am early in that journey and I say so. But I am the kind of person who learns a thing by building it, which is exactly why this page exists.

Everything you are looking at right now — the layout, the colours, the code, the animation — was written by me. Not downloaded, not generated from a theme, not dragged and dropped in a page builder.

Built by me

This website was designed and coded by me.

Katlego Khumo Matabane — that is me — designed this site and wrote every line of the code behind it. No template, no theme, no website builder. It is a single HTML file with hand-written CSS and vanilla JavaScript.

I did this on purpose. A portfolio that uses a builder proves you can pick a template. Building it from scratch proves you can actually do the work — the layout, the responsiveness, the accessibility, the little animations, the way it behaves when you shrink the window down to a phone.

  • Written by hand in HTML5, CSS3 and vanilla JavaScript — no frameworks, no libraries, no dependencies.
  • That rotating 3D portrait is mine as well: my own WebGL renderer, my own matrix maths, my own GLSL shaders. No three.js. Check the triangle and fps counter under it.
  • One single file — no build step, no dependencies, nothing to install. It works offline and on a cheap phone.
  • Responsive from a 320 px screen up to a desktop, with a keyboard-accessible menu and reduced-motion support.
  • Right-click → View page source. Every rule and every function is mine, and it is commented in plain language.
